Manipulation in simulation

SimulationJan 2025 – May 2026· Solo project

These are simulation projects where I worked on perception and planning for arms: colour sorting with a Franka Panda, two Pandas playing tic-tac-toe, a mobile manipulator, and gesture control of a UR5. The SCARA build is where I took this onto real hardware.

Two Franka Pandas playing tic-tac-toe

Oct – Nov 2025

  • Board and cubes designed in Fusion 360; an overhead OpenCV camera reads the board.
  • MoveIt 2 plans both arms; an AI decision node plays Human vs AI and AI vs AI.

Post, Nov 2025

Franka Panda colour sorting

Sep – Oct 2025 · taught as a masterclass on 5 Oct 2025

  • OpenCV HSV segmentation, tf2 transforms into the robot frame, MoveIt 2 through pymoveit2.
  • ros2_control trajectory and gripper controllers in Gazebo, with a Docker image for Humble and Jazzy.
  • My most-used open-source project. Viewers reproduced it on their own arms and in Isaac Sim.
  • Limit: a single RGB camera with a fixed pick height, not a depth camera.

Workshop recording

Aura, a mobile manipulator

Dec 2024 – Feb 2025

  • A differential-drive base in Gazebo with an arm that started as 4-DOF and ended as 5-DOF.
  • Nav2, MoveIt, frontier-based exploration, an ArUco docking station, colour detection and Alexa voice control.

Video Post, Jan 2025

UR5 gesture pick-and-place

May 2026

  • MediaPipe hand tracking drives a UR5 with a Robotiq gripper in Gazebo.
  • Limit: it sends joint targets directly to the trajectory controller, with no MoveIt planning loop.
